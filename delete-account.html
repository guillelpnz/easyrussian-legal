Delete your Easy Russian account and data

Last updated: September 11, 2026

You can permanently delete your Easy Russian account and all the data we hold about you in two ways.

Option 1 — From inside the app (instant)

  1. Open Easy Russian on your device.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Go to the Profile tab.
  4. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm. Your account, learning history, streaks, reviews and any uploaded content are erased immediately.

Option 2 — By email (within 30 days)

If you no longer have access to the app, email us and we will delete your account manually.

Send an email to: guillelupianez99@gmail.com

Subject: Delete my Easy Russian account

Body: Include the email address you used to sign up so we can locate your account.

We will confirm the deletion by replying to that same email within 30 days, and usually within 2 business days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record (email, authentication metadata).
  • Your learning progress (cards reviewed, streaks, XP, daily stats, league standings).
  • Any custom flashcards you created.
  • Any messages you sent to the in-app AI tutor.
  • Your purchase entitlement record (subscription history retained by Apple, Google and our payment processor RevenueCat for accounting and tax purposes — see our Privacy Policy).

What we keep

We may retain anonymised, aggregated usage statistics that cannot be linked back to you, and the minimum information required to comply with our legal and tax obligations (typically subscription transaction records for up to 7 years, as required by Spanish law).

Data retention

Once you trigger deletion, your personal data is removed from our active systems immediately. Encrypted backups containing the data are rotated out within 30 days.
